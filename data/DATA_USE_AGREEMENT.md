# ProAct Data Use Agreement

These terms govern access to and use of the files provided through the ProAct controlled-access dataset release. They apply to each approved researcher individually. See [Dataset Access](DATASET_ACCESS.md) for the application process and current availability.

The repository's code is licensed under [Apache License 2.0](../LICENSE). That license does **not** cover the dataset. Access to the dataset is subject to the terms below.

## 1. Research-only use

Use the dataset only for non-commercial scientific research, including developing and evaluating research methods and reporting scientific findings. Do not use the dataset for products, commercial services, or other commercial exploitation without prior written permission.

Approval covers the research purpose described in your application. Contact the dataset maintainers before using the data for a different purpose that falls outside that approval.

## 2. Privacy

Do not identify or attempt to identify people in the dataset, link the data to identifying information, or contact people depicted in the data. Do not reverse or attempt to reverse privacy transformations, including anonymization, masking, blurring, or removal of identifying information.

Protect downloaded data and any derived material from access by people who have not been individually approved. If you discover identifying information or a privacy concern, report it privately to [zhuxiaomeng224@gmail.com](mailto:zhuxiaomeng224@gmail.com) without posting the affected material publicly.

## 3. Individual access and no redistribution

Each researcher must apply using their own Hugging Face account and receive individual approval. Approval for one researcher does not grant access to their colleagues, collaborators, students, or institution.

Do not redistribute the dataset, publish mirrors, share download links that bypass access controls, or share account credentials or access tokens without prior written permission. These restrictions also cover dataset copies and derived material that exposes restricted content, such as extracted frames, video clips, or reconstructed records.

## 4. Research outputs

You may publish research results, analyses, and aggregate evaluation metrics, provided they respect these privacy and redistribution terms. Do not include restricted dataset content or material that enables identification or reconstruction of that content in public papers, supplements, demonstrations, repositories, or other outputs without prior written permission.

## 5. Exceptions and violations

Request any exception, including commercial use or redistribution, in advance at [zhuxiaomeng224@gmail.com](mailto:zhuxiaomeng224@gmail.com). An exception requires written permission; submitting a request does not grant it.

Violations may result in revocation of future access and refusal of future requests. Revoking access prevents further authorized downloads; it does not remotely recover or erase files that have already been downloaded. These use restrictions continue to apply to retained copies.
